## Step 2: Understand the Brand Identity

### Who

- **Podcast:** Hostfulness — with Rok Kokalj
- **Publisher:** Nevron (tech company, 20+ years in hospitality tech)
- **Host:** Rok Kokalj, founder of Nevron. Coined the word "Hostfulness."
- **Handle:** @RokKokaljHost
- **Language:** Slovenian (primary), English (secondary — for international reach)

### What Hostfulness Means

Hostfulness is NOT a hospitality skill. It is a way of being.

> Hostfulness is the practice of showing up for others with presence, respect and genuine care — in every relationship, not just in hospitality.

It is mindfulness turned outward — toward another person.

### Tone of Voice

**Sproščen, a globok.** Relaxed, but deep.

- Not academic, not corporate, not motivational-speaker
- Direct, no hedging — but never cold
- Respectful toward guests and toward the subject matter
- **Warmth. Depth. Authenticity. Invitation.**

Tone keywords in order of priority:
1. **Warm** — human, genuine, caring
2. **Deep** — thoughtful, layered, not surface-level
3. **Authentic** — no pretense, no marketing speak, no hype
4. **Inviting** — draws people in, never lectures or preaches

### What the Tone is NOT

- Not salesy or promotional
- Not motivational/inspirational poster energy
- Not academic or theoretical
- Not corporate/formal
- Not cynical or edgy
- Not preachy or moralizing

### Visual Mood

- Dark, cinematic backgrounds — deep navy to black gradients
- Warm gold as the accent — never as background fill
- Photography: natural, studio-quality, warm lighting on the host
- Clean, generous whitespace — not cluttered
- Premium feel without being luxury/pretentious
- The gold suggests depth and warmth, not wealth or opulence

### Design Rules

1. **Logo hierarchy:** "hostfulness" logo is always the dominant brand mark. Nevron appears secondary/subtle.
2. **Gold is accent only.** Never use gold as a large background fill. It lives in the logo, in subtle highlights, and in small accent elements.
3. **Dark backgrounds are the canvas.** The navy-to-black gradient is the foundation of every visual.
4. **Photography over illustration.** Real people, real moments. No stock-photo energy.
5. **Script "with Rok Kokalj" is part of the logo.** Always appears with the wordmark, positioned below.
6. **Breathing room.** Compositions should feel open, not packed. Generous margins.
7. **No neon, no saturated colors, no busy patterns.** The palette is intentionally restrained.

## Step 4: Cardinal Rules

These are non-negotiable. Every piece of content must respect them:

1. **Through the product, we seek the person.** Properties, services, offers are part of the conversation — but through them we always seek the relationship the host builds with the guest.
2. **We do not promote transactional hospitality.** We do not glorify the industry for its own sake. The industry is context, not the goal.
3. **We always seek genuine hospitality** — moments when a person transcends their role, when a relationship transcends a transaction.
4. **Hospitality is not limited to the hospitality industry.** It is a broader social concept — a kind, dignified, respectful relationship between people, in all domains.

## Step 5: Produce Content

When the user invokes this skill, ask what they need if not already specified. Common outputs:

### Written Content
- **Episode descriptions** (Spotify/Apple) — SLO + EN, following the official About tone
- **Social media posts** — warm, inviting, never salesy. Tease the human story, not the property
- **Episode intros/outros** — spoken-word scripts in the Hostfulness tone
- **Newsletter copy** — same tone, deeper format
- **Pull quotes** — extract the most human, genuine moments from episode content
- **YouTube titles & descriptions** — discoverable but not clickbait

### Design Briefs
- **Thumbnail briefs** — specify layout, text, which assets to use, mood reference
- **Social graphics briefs** — dimensions, copy placement, color usage, asset references
- **Banner/header briefs** — following the existing visual language

### Content Rules for All Outputs

**Language:**
- Default to Slovenian unless the user specifies English or both
- When bilingual, SLO first, then EN
- Match the manifesto tone — direct, warm, deep, never corporate

**Structure:**
- Lead with the human story, not the property or credentials
- If mentioning a property, frame it as context for the hospitality story
- End with an invitation (to listen, to reflect, to engage) — never a hard CTA

**Hashtags & Tags (when applicable):**
- `#hostfulness` (always)
- `#gostoljubje` (SLO content)
- `#hospitality` (EN content)
- `#podcast` (when relevant)
- Guest/property tags as provided by the user

**What to NEVER produce:**
- Corporate buzzword copy ("synergy," "leverage," "ecosystem")
- Hype language ("AMAZING episode!", "You WON'T believe...")
- Content that glorifies luxury, exclusivity, or status
- Content that reduces hospitality to service quality metrics
- Anything preachy or moralizing — the tone is an invitation, not a lecture
- **Absolutely no em dashes (—).** Use a regular hyphen-space ( - ) or rephrase the sentence instead. This applies to all outputs — no exceptions.
