# Hostfulness Podcast — Manifesto

## Core Concept

Besedo "gostoljubje" vsi poznamo. Uporabljamo jo ohlapno — kot sinonim za prijaznost, ustrežljivost, dobro postrežbo. Ampak gostoljubje je veliko vec od tega.

Je eden najstarejsih medcloveskih odnosov. Dobesedno pomeni ljubiti gosta. Ne samo postreci mu. Ne samo tolerirati ga. Pomeni izrazati najboljsi del sebe — tudi do neznanca.

Pomeni odreci se delu svojega udobja, svoje suverenosti, svoje svobode. Sprejeti odgovornost za drugega cloveka — za njegovo varnost, pocutje, za dobro porabljen cas, ki ti ga je zaupal.

In pomeni, da ta odgovornost ni enostranska. Gostitelj daje. Gost spoštuje meje. To je razmerje, ne storitev.

## Transakcijsko in pristno gostoljubje

Ko gostoljubje postane pridobitna dejavnost, se globina pogosto izgubi. Nastane transakcijsko gostoljubje — storitev proti placilu. Gost dobi sobo, obrok, profesionalno postrežbo. Legitimna menjava. Temelj celotne hospitality industrije.

Ampak transakcija sama po sebi ne zahteva cloveškega odnosa. Lahko dobiš perfektno storitev in se ob tem ne počutiš sprejet. Lahko je vse brezhibno — in vseeno prazno.

Transakcijsko gostoljubje izpolni pricakovanja. Redko jih preseze.

Pristno gostoljubje se zgodi, ko clovek za storitvijo preseze transakcijo. Ko ni vec samo ponudnik, ampak postane gostitelj. Ko pozornost ni naucena, ampak iskrena. Ko gost zacuti, da ni le stevilka, ampak da je bil resnicno sprejet — kot clovek, ne kot stranka.

Pristno gostoljubje ne potrebuje industrije. Obstaja povsod, kjer se en clovek zavestno obrne k drugemu z dobronamernostjo, spoštovanjem in prisotnostjo (avtenticnost). Je starejse od hotelov, od turizma, od vsake poslovne panoge.

Pristno, iskreno gostoljubje je cloveški odnos v svoji najcistejsi obliki.

## Gostoljubje kot širši družbeni koncept

Gostoljubje ni omejeno na hotele. Je dobronameren, korekten, dostojanstven in spoštljiv odnos — na vseh podrocjih medcloveskih odnosov.

- Dobronameren odnos — v casu polnem manipulacije.
- Dostojanstven odnos — v casu polnem ponizevanja.
- Spoštljiv odnos — v casu prezetem z zanicevanjem.

## What is Hostfulness

Verjetno poznate besedo mindfulness — zavestna prisotnost do sebe, do lastnih misli in custev.

**Hostfulness je mindfulness, obrnjen navzven — k drugemu cloveku.**

Hostfulness je neologizem. Nova beseda, ki jo je skoval Rok Kokalj, ustanovitelj podjetja Nevron in voditelj tega podcasta.

**Hostfulness is the practice of showing up for others with presence, respect and genuine care — in every relationship, not just in hospitality.**

Hostfulness ni hospitality skill. Je nacin bivanja — kako se zavestno obrnemo k drugemu cloveku v kateremkoli odnosu.

Vsakic ko smo v stiku z drugim clovekom, smo bodisi gostitelj bodisi gost.

- Ko gost vstopi v hotelsko avlo — ocitno.
- Ko nov zaposleni pride v podjetje.
- Ko pacient vstopi v bolnisnico.
- Ko uporabnik odpre tvojo aplikacijo.
- Ko se otrok vrne iz sole.
- Ko sosed potrka na vrata.

V vseh teh trenutkih je nekdo sprejet. Nekdo vstopa v prostor drugega — fizicen, digitalen ali custven. Vprasanje je vedno isto: **Kako ga sprejmemo?**

## About the Podcast

Hostfulness je podcast o gostoljubju kot nacinu bivanja.

Sprosceni, globoki pogovori z ljudmi, ki jim je gostoljubje strast — v poklicu ali v zivljenju. Vecinoma s hotelirji in gostitelji, obcasno z drugimi strokovnjaki in osebami, ki v svojem delu ali vsakdanjem zivljenju zavestno gostijo druge.

Govorimo o konkretnih stvareh — o namestitvi, ponudbi, ekipi, izzivih, poti sogovornika. Namestitev je produkt in ponos hotelirja, in je pomemben del pogovora. Ampak skozi produkt iscemo cloveka. Namestitev je kontekst, v katerem se gostoljubje dogaja.

Prakticni pogovori z globljim podtonom. Vedno iscemo trenutke, ko gostoljubje preseze transakcijo, ko se pokaze pristni cloveški odnos.

## Three Thematic Axes

### 1. Gostoljubje kot koncept
Kaj gostoljubje v resnici je. Ne kot industrija, ampak kot eden najstarejsih odnosov med ljudmi.

### 2. Gostoljubje z namenom — Intentional Hospitality
Zavestno ostajati v stanju iskrenega gostoljubja, ne pasti v avtopilot. In vprasanje namena — zakaj gostimo, katere potrebe gosta zelimo izpolniti. Gost naj bi iz namestitve odsel vsaj malo boljsega pocutja, kot je prisel. Cetudi samo dobro naspan.

Nameni bivanja se raztezajo od osnovnega — pocitek, varnost, udobje — do globljega: odmik od vsakodnevnih obveznosti, obnovitev notranjih energij, regulacija pocutja, sirsitev zavesti prek novih odnosov in kultur.

### 3. From Hospitality to Better Humanity
Namestitveni objekti so centri, skozi katere se izmenja ogromno stevilo ljudi. Imajo moznost zavestnega pozitivnega vpliva na goste — ze samo z izpolnjevanjem vloge dobronamernega, iskrenega gostitelja. Ce gost s sabo odnese eno samo dobro, pozitivno izkusnjo, lahko ta pozitivno vpliva na njegovo zivljenje in zivljenja drugih okoli njega.

## Why Nevron

Nevron je tech podjetje, ki razvija digitalne resitve za interakcijo med namestitvami in gosti. Vec kot 20 let deluje kot zunanji opazovalec hospitality industrije — ne kot insider, ampak kot nekdo, ki ze desetletja gleda od zunaj.

Ravno ta pozicija sili k drugacnemu pogledu. Nevron se mora stalno sprasevati: kako s pomocjo digitalnih storitev povecati ali izboljsati gostoljubje? To vprasanje sili k razumevanju, kaj gostoljubje v resnici je — ne samo kaj industrija misli, da je.

Nevron vidi hotele in namestitve kot katalizatorje — tocke, skozi katere gredo ljudje in iz katerih lahko odidejo vsaj malo boljsi, kot so prisli.

## Cardinal Rules

These apply to ALL content that carries the Hostfulness name:

1. **Skozi produkt iscemo cloveka.** Namestitev, ponudba, storitev so del pogovora — ampak skozi njih vedno iscemo odnos, ki ga gostitelj gradi z gostom.
2. **Ne promoviramo transakcijskega gostoljubja.** Ne glorificiramo industrije same po sebi. Industrija je kontekst, ne cilj.
3. **Vedno iscemo pristno gostoljubje** — trenutke, ko clovek preseze vlogo, ko odnos preseze transakcijo.
4. **Gostoljubje ni omejeno na hospitality.** Je sirsi druzbeni koncept — dobronameren, dostojanstven, spoštljiv odnos med ljudmi, na vseh podrocjih.

# Hostfulness Podcast — Manifesto (English Version)

## Core Concept

We all know the word hospitality. We use it loosely — as a synonym for friendliness, attentiveness, good service. But hospitality is much more than that.

It is one of the oldest human relationships. It literally means to love the guest. Not just serve them. Not just tolerate them. It means expressing the best part of yourself — even toward a stranger.

It means giving up part of your comfort, your sovereignty, your freedom. Accepting responsibility for another person — for their safety, their well-being, for the time they have entrusted to you.

And it means that this responsibility is not one-sided. The host gives. The guest respects boundaries. It is a relationship, not a service.

## Transactional vs. Genuine Hospitality

When hospitality becomes a commercial activity, its depth is often lost. What emerges is transactional hospitality — service in exchange for payment. The guest receives a room, a meal, professional service. A legitimate exchange. The foundation of the entire hospitality industry.

But a transaction in itself does not require a human connection. You can receive perfect service and still not feel welcome. Everything can be flawless — and still feel empty.

Transactional hospitality meets expectations. It rarely exceeds them.

Genuine hospitality happens when the person behind the service transcends the transaction. When they are no longer just a provider, but become a host. When attention is not learned, but sincere. When the guest feels they are not just a number, but that they were truly welcomed — as a person, not as a customer.

Genuine hospitality does not need an industry. It exists wherever one person consciously turns toward another with goodwill, respect, and presence. It is older than hotels, older than tourism, older than any business sector.

Genuine, sincere hospitality is the human relationship in its purest form.

## Hospitality as a Broader Social Concept

Hospitality is not limited to hotels. It is a kind, correct, dignified, and respectful relationship — in all areas of human interaction.

- A kind relationship — in a time full of manipulation.
- A dignified relationship — in a time full of humiliation.
- A respectful relationship — in a time steeped in contempt.

## What is Hostfulness

You probably know the word mindfulness — conscious presence toward yourself, toward your own thoughts and feelings.

**Hostfulness is mindfulness turned outward — toward another person.**

Hostfulness is a neologism. A new word, coined by Rok Kokalj, founder of Nevron and host of this podcast.

**Hostfulness is the practice of showing up for others with presence, respect and genuine care — in every relationship, not just in hospitality.**

Hostfulness is not a hospitality skill. It is a way of being — how we consciously turn toward another person in any relationship.

Every time we are in contact with another person, we are either a host or a guest.

- When a guest walks into a hotel lobby — obviously.
- When a new employee arrives at a company.
- When a patient enters a hospital.
- When a user opens your application.
- When a child comes home from school.
- When a neighbor knocks on the door.

In all these moments, someone is being received. Someone is entering another's space — physical, digital, or emotional. The question is always the same: **How do we receive them?**

## About the Podcast

Hostfulness is a podcast about hospitality as a way of being.

Relaxed, deep conversations with people for whom hospitality is a passion — in their profession or in their life. Mostly with hoteliers and hosts, occasionally with other experts and individuals who consciously host others in their work or everyday life.

We talk about concrete things — about the property, the offering, the team, challenges, the guest's journey. The property is the product and the pride of the hotelier, and it is an important part of the conversation. But through the product, we seek the person. The property is the context in which hospitality happens.

Practical conversations with a deeper undertone. We always look for moments when hospitality transcends the transaction, when genuine human connection reveals itself.

## Three Thematic Axes

### 1. Hospitality as a Concept
What hospitality truly is. Not as an industry, but as one of the oldest relationships between people.

### 2. Intentional Hospitality
Consciously remaining in a state of genuine hospitality, not falling into autopilot. And the question of intent — why do we host, which needs of the guest do we wish to fulfill. The guest should leave the property feeling at least a little better than when they arrived. Even if just well-rested.

The purposes of a stay range from the basic — rest, safety, comfort — to the deeper: a break from daily obligations, renewal of inner energy, emotional regulation, expansion of awareness through new relationships and cultures.

### 3. From Hospitality to Better Humanity
Accommodation properties are centers through which a vast number of people pass. They have the opportunity for conscious positive impact on guests — simply by fulfilling the role of a kind, sincere host. If a guest takes away even one good, positive experience, it can positively influence their life and the lives of others around them.

## Why Nevron

Nevron is a tech company that develops digital solutions for the interaction between accommodation properties and guests. For more than 20 years, it has operated as an external observer of the hospitality industry — not as an insider, but as someone who has been looking in from the outside for decades.

This very position forces a different perspective. Nevron must constantly ask itself: how can we use digital services to increase or improve hospitality? This question forces an understanding of what hospitality truly is — not just what the industry thinks it is.

Nevron sees hotels and properties as catalysts — points through which people pass and from which they can leave at least a little better than when they arrived.

## Cardinal Rules

These apply to ALL content that carries the Hostfulness name:

1. **Through the product, we seek the person.** Properties, offers, services are part of the conversation — but through them we always seek the relationship the host builds with the guest.
2. **We do not promote transactional hospitality.** We do not glorify the industry for its own sake. The industry is context, not the goal.
3. **We always seek genuine hospitality** — moments when a person transcends their role, when a relationship transcends a transaction.
4. **Hospitality is not limited to the hospitality industry.** It is a broader social concept — a kind, dignified, respectful relationship between people, in all domains.
